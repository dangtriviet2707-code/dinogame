# Viet Legends v1.7.9 – Nộ Hải cooldown & mobile controls

- Nộ Hải cooldown reduced from 1800 frames (30 seconds at 60 FPS) to 600 frames (10 seconds).
- On touch devices, moved the Nộ Hải button farther left from the dodge button and raised its stacking order to keep the touch target separate.
- Kept skill damage, cast delay, effects, unlock requirements, and other skill cooldowns unchanged.
